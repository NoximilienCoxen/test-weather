Caelum Weather is the sky, drawn. A weather app with no ads, no accounts and no tracking, showing the weather for any place in the world under a sky that changes with the forecast.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Now and the next 24 hours, scrubbed with your thumb
• The week, day by day: press and hold a day to preview it
• What to wear when you go out, in one line
• Rain in the next minutes, by the quarter hour
• Rain: when it arrives, how much, and for how long
• Official weather warnings from national weather services (in Europe through MeteoAlarm), kept apart from the notices the app works out by itself
• Air quality and pollen
• Wind, gusts and direction
• The sea, with its own page for coastal places only: waves, tides and water temperature
• UV index through the day, with the WHO category and advice
• Moon: the phase, day by day, in three dimensions
• Pressure, dew point, sunshine hours, golden hour and blue hour
• Your saved places compared side by side

OUTSIDE THE APP
• Nine resizable home screen widgets: weather, hours, week, moon, air, wind, sea, mini and one that fits any size
• Notifications for rain or hail on the way, with a countdown, and for orange and red warnings
• "Take an umbrella": in the morning, only on rainy days
• A notice when tomorrow's or the next day's forecast really changes
• Morning summary, at the time you choose
• Personal notices: frost, heat, strong wind, rain and UV, at the threshold you choose
• Quick Settings tile and launcher shortcuts
• Wear OS watch app, with Tile and complication
• On tablets and foldables: the sky on one side, the pages on the other

HOW IT TREATS YOUR DATA
Only the coordinates of the place shown leave the phone, to request the forecast. No account, no usage statistics, no ads. Location is optional and approximate only.

In English, Italian, German, French and Spanish.

Weather data by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0). Air quality: Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service. Sea: Copernicus Marine Service, ECMWF, NOAA NCEP, DWD. Warnings: MeteoAlarm and national weather services. None of the sources endorses this app.
