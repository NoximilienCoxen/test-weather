Caelum Weather is the sky, drawn. A weather app with no ads, no accounts and no tracking, showing the weather for any place in the world under a sky that changes with the forecast.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Now and the next 24 hours, scrubbed with your thumb
• The week, day by day
• Rain: when it arrives, how much, and for how long
• Official weather warnings from national weather services (in Europe through MeteoAlarm), kept apart from the notices the app works out by itself
• Air quality and pollen
• Wind, gusts and direction
• UV index through the day
• Moon: the phase, day by day, in three dimensions
• Pressure, dew point, sunshine hours, golden hour and blue hour
• Your saved places compared side by side

OUTSIDE THE APP
• Six home screen widgets: weather, hours, week, moon, air, mini
• Notifications for rain or hail on the way and for orange and red warnings
• Morning summary, at the time you choose
• Quick Settings tile and launcher shortcuts
• Wear OS watch app, with Tile and complication

HOW IT TREATS YOUR DATA
Only the coordinates of the place shown leave the phone, to request the forecast. No account, no usage statistics, no ads. Location is optional and approximate only.

In English and Italian.

Weather data by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0). Air quality: Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service. Warnings: MeteoAlarm and national weather services. None of the sources endorses this app.
